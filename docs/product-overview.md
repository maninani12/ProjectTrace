# Product overview

ProjectTrace checks engineering statements against source evidence and links inconsistencies to changes, owners and review decisions. An evidence graph unifies claims, source artifacts, static findings and dependencies. Every conclusion has a snapshot and analyzer version.

The primary investigation starts in Overview, opens a claim, follows its evidence/history, inspects the PR gate and records a reasoned review. Claims, findings and review state are separate. Use the CEO demo guide for a 3–5 minute narrative.

Native rule coverage is deliberately limited and disclosed. External providers can supply additional evidence, but the local demo does not show them as connected. No commercial pricing, active billing plans or invented cost estimates are displayed.
