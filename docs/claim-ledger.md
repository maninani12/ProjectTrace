# Claim Ledger

Supported deterministic templates extract atomic authentication, technology, health-route and event-transport statements from markdown. Compound technology declarations are split. Supported AST evidence can verify a route or authentication configuration. Imports and pinned dependencies support INFERRED results; unsupported statements remain UNVERIFIED.

JWT → session fixture evidence produces a scoped CONTRADICTED result. Changed evidence adds a STALE history step before the new verdict. No state is called VERIFIED without a snapshot reference. Severity, confidence, verification, review status and owner are independent fields.

The inspector shows document source, supporting/contradicting evidence, scope, history and recommendation. Human confirmation does not override the analyzer's truth verdict. Broad semantic extraction, arbitrary claim templates and runtime validation remain deferred.
