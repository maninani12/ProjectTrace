11. CLAIM STATUS
======================================================================

Use:

VERIFIED

Strong current evidence directly supports the claim.

INFERRED

Strong indirect evidence exists but cannot conclusively prove the
statement.

UNVERIFIED

Insufficient evidence.

CONTRADICTED

Current evidence conflicts with the claim.

STALE

Claim was previously supported or associated evidence changed and
requires reverification.

If evidence directly disproves the statement:

use CONTRADICTED.
