======================================================================
9. CLAIM STATUSES
======================================================================

Use exactly:

VERIFIED

Current strong evidence directly supports the claim.

INFERRED

Available evidence strongly suggests the claim but does not prove it.

UNVERIFIED

Insufficient evidence.

CONTRADICTED

Current evidence conflicts with the claim.

STALE

Relevant supporting evidence changed or the claim has not been
re-established against the current snapshot.

Status is NOT:

severity

confidence

review state.

Keep those separate.
