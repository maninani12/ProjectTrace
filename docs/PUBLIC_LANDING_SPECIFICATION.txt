######################################################################
# PROJECTTRACE — MASTER PUBLIC LANDING PAGE SPECIFICATION
######################################################################

IMPORTANT:

ProjectTrace requires a world-class PUBLIC LANDING PAGE in addition to
the authenticated application.

The landing page is NOT:

- the ProjectTrace dashboard
- an admin screen
- a collection of feature cards
- a college-project homepage
- a generic SaaS template
- an AI-generated purple-gradient website
- a copy of Linear, Vercel, Wiz, SonarQube, GitHub or another company

The landing page represents the PRODUCT and COMPANY before a visitor
logs in.

It must be suitable for:

- developers
- startup founders
- CTOs
- CEOs
- engineering managers
- platform teams
- security engineers
- investors
- evaluators
- non-technical visitors

A person who has NEVER heard of ProjectTrace should understand:

WHAT it is

WHAT problem it solves

HOW it works

WHY it is different

WHAT it can analyze

WHAT result it gives

WHY they should care

WHAT they should do next

within the first few minutes of visiting the site.

The product must explain itself.
